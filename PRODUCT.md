# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing codebase: Vite, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind v4, motion, shadcn/ui on Radix. Deployed static (Vercel). Content lives in typed files under `src/data/`.

## Users

Recruiters and internship leads, plus small-business owners looking for freelance web and design work. They arrive from a resume, a LinkedIn profile or a link in an application, mostly on a phone, and decide in under a minute whether to read further or reach out.

## Product Purpose

A one-page personal portfolio for Lalaine Jannah dela Cruz, a 4th year BS Information Technology student who designs the visuals and ships the code herself. Success is a recruiter reaching the work, the credentials and the contact details quickly, and remembering that she does both design and development.

## Positioning

She designs it, then builds it: the same person made the print and social material (50+ pieces) and the coded client websites. Few interns show both.

## Operating Context

Looking for a remote 3 to 6 month internship starting December 2026. Takes freelance design and web work. The audience reads the resume first and the portfolio second.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sections are content, not features: overview, then a folder of tabbed material (works, experience, credentials, education, achievements, about, contact).
- Must work on a phone first: the folder is portrait with sideways tabs on mobile, landscape with top tabs on desktop.
- Light and dark theme, light by default.
- No phone number or street address is published. Email, LinkedIn and GitHub only.
- Certificate verification links: 5 of 23 are unresolved and render without a Verify button (see CERTIFICATE-LINKS.md).
- Design piece titles are inferred from filenames and still need correcting.

## Brand Commitments

- Palette is pinned by the owner: #6B35E8, #3F13A4, #050505, #595959, #FAFAFA.
- Hero is her name set in two different typefaces.
- Overview places her photo, background removed, overlapping the word "Overview".
- Third section is one large folder with tabs. Landscape on desktop with tabs on top; portrait on mobile with tabs on the right edge and the text running sideways.
- Flat and minimal, with a magazine feel. Animation that follows the scroll.

## Evidence on Hand

- Resume: `assets/information/Lalaine_Dela_Cruz_Resume_GAO_Web_Developer_Intern.pdf` (education, three roles, one capstone project, awards, toolkit).
- 23 certificates as images (`assets/certificates/`), 11 design pieces (`assets/creative_projects/`), 2 live client sites with screenshots (`assets/websites/`), a studio portrait (`assets/lalaine_profile_photo.png`).
- Absent: testimonials, client logos, a project case study beyond the resume bullets. None may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Show the work before talking about it.
2. Every claim comes from the resume or the repository. Numbers are quoted, never rounded up.
3. One memorable object (the folder), everything else quiet.
4. A phone visitor gets the same content and the same delight as a desktop one.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Body text 4.5:1 in both themes, keyboard-operable tabs with visible focus, reduced-motion respected, 44px minimum touch targets.
